Our story

An idea that became a company.

Around three decades back from today the idea of Gazal Al Khadra Trading was conceived by the visionary and assiduous management. Very soon the idea became reality, and in the year 1996 the company started its first commercial production.

Gazal Al Kadhara Trading is a major player in the Sultanate of Oman’s spices and pulses market, and by now we have a leading market position in categories like spices, pulses, dry fruits, nuts and Omani coffee.

All of Gazal’s products are traded under a single brand — Noor Gazal — and are directly supplied to the stores across all the governorates of Oman.

Over the years Noor Gazal has attained significant market share by way of offering exceptionally high quality products, and has become one of the most favoured household brands of the Sultanate of Oman.

Quality

Certified, and held to.

Being an ISO 22000:2005 and HACCP certified company, Noor Gazal ensures the highest level of hygiene and sanitary conditions.

ISO 22000:2005

A food safety management system

Certification covering the process end to end, from intake through to the sealed pack that leaves the facility.

HACCP

Hazard analysis and critical control points

The internationally recognised system for identifying food safety hazards and controlling them at the stages where it matters.

Hygiene

The highest level, maintained

Hygiene and sanitary conditions held to across the factory floor, as the certification requires.
