Our story

Three decades in the same trade.

Gazal Al Khadara Trading was conceived around three decades ago and began commercial production in 1996. Since then we have grown into one of the leading names in the Sultanate's spices and pulses market.

Everything we make is traded under a single brand — Noor Gazal — and supplied directly to stores across all the governorates of Oman. One name on every pack means there is nowhere for a bad batch to hide, which is exactly the point.

The trade rewards patience. We buy from the crops we trust, at the time of year they are worth buying, and we would rather be short of a line for a few weeks than fill it with something we would not cook with at home.

Our ambition is straightforward: keep earning the place we hold in Omani kitchens, and take that same standard to markets beyond.

Quality

Certified, and checked anyway.

Our processing facility sits at Kadhara in the Wilayat of Suwaiq, equipped with state-of-the-art commodity handling equipment.

ISO 22000:2005

A food safety system, not a certificate on a wall

Hazards identified at every step from intake to sealing, with the controls written down and the records kept.

HACCP

Critical points, monitored

The stages where something can actually go wrong are the stages we measure, and a reading outside the limit stops the line.

Hygiene

Sanitary conditions, maintained daily

The highest level of hygiene and sanitary conditions across the factory floor — held to by routine, not by inspection week.

Sourcing

The finest crops, and nothing added

Products manufactured from the best available crops, with no additives — the simplest promise to make and the hardest to keep at scale.
