kambio. The problem

Right now, the exporter is the integration layer.

After an order is confirmed, moving a shipment forward is mostly one person moving documents and answers between people who cannot see each other's systems.

Email

Buyer

Sends the PO, then chases for the invoice, packing list and B/L in a thread that has drifted twelve replies deep.

WhatsApp

CHA

Wants HS codes and invoice values again, in a slightly different format, before the checklist can be filed.

Email

Forwarder

Needs weights, ports and dates — none of which live anywhere except in someone's head and three attachments.

And in the middle

A human copy-paste bridge. The same PO number typed four times, the same HS code re-checked in three threads, and one version of the truth that exists only because somebody remembers it.

What that costs

  • Hours a day spent relaying rather than selling or sourcing.
  • Errors that survive because nobody re-reads what they retyped.
  • Documents reaching parties who should never have received them.
  • History that walks out of the door when a person leaves.

kambio. How it works

Four steps, starting from an email you already received.

Kambio does not ask your buyer or your CHA to change anything. It sits behind the channels you already use and turns them into structure.

1

Forward the email

Send a buyer enquiry or purchase order to your Kambio address. No integration project, no data migration, no change to how your buyer writes to you.

2

Kambio builds the shipment

PO number, line items, HS codes, incoterms, amounts and parties are extracted automatically — each field carrying a confidence score and the verbatim source line it came from.

3

Bring in the counterparties

Your CHA, freight forwarder and buyer get a scoped link into the shipment room. No signup, no licence, no new app — they keep using email and WhatsApp.

4

You confirm, Kambio relays

AI drafts the replies and the relay messages between parties. A human always confirms before anything goes out. Nothing financially consequential is sent by AI.

The buyer asks for a change on the bill of lading. Kambio knows the forwarder issues that document, writes the message to the forwarder carrying the buyer's words, and puts it in front of you to send. You never open a second thread.

kambio. The shipment room

One room per shipment. Four parties. Four different views.

Every shipment is a permissioned room shared by the exporter, the CHA, the freight forwarder and the buyer. Each party sees only the documents they are entitled to.

Who sees what

Default policy
Document Exporter CHA Forwarder Buyer
Purchase order ✓ — — ✓
Commercial invoice & packing list ✓ ✓ — ✓
CHA checklist ✓ ✓ — —
Shipping bill ✓ ✓ ✓ —
Phytosanitary / fumigation / origin ✓ ✓ — ✓
BL draft & bill of lading ✓ ✓ ✓ ✓

These are the defaults, derived from the document type. You can widen or narrow any individual document.

Enforced, not advisory

Visibility is applied in the database query, not in the interface. A document a party may not see is absent from their results — not hidden by a filter that could be bypassed. Kambio refuses outright to ask a party to review something they are not entitled to.

Fails closed

An unclassified document is visible to the exporter alone until someone decides otherwise. When extraction re-classifies an upload — recognising it as a checklist, say — the policy is re-applied immediately.

kambio. What each party gets

Nobody has to adopt anything.

Only the exporter has an account. Everyone else takes part through a scoped link, or simply keeps replying to email as they always have.

Exporter

Stops being the bridge

One workspace per shipment instead of four threads. Fields extracted with their sources so figures are checkable, not retyped. A queue that opens on what is blocked today rather than a list of everything. Drafted replies waiting for a decision, not a blank compose window.

CHA

Asks once, gets an answer

A link to exactly one shipment, showing the documents relevant to clearing it. Upload the checklist and the shipping bill straight into the room. Request changes and have them reach the right person without a phone call. No login, no software.

Freight forwarder

Has what they need, and no more

The shipping bill needed to release the bill of lading, and nothing from the exporter's internal working papers. Upload the BL draft where everyone entitled to it can see it. A different forwarder every sailing is normal and costs nothing to set up.

Buyer

Stops asking "where is it?"

A read-only link showing live status, route and dates, plus the documents they are entitled to. No signup. They never see a shipping bill or a customs checklist — not because someone remembered to trim recipients, but because the room does not contain them.

One thread per shipment, per party

Every message Kambio sends a party continues a single email chain, with a stable reference-prefixed subject. One shipment is one conversation — not five subject lines to search through six weeks later. When a party goes quiet on a review request, Kambio chases them: capped, and it stops the moment they reply.

kambio. Trust & architecture

AI drafts. People decide. The record keeps itself.

Export operations carry money and legal exposure. Kambio is built so that automation never becomes an unaccountable actor in the business.

A human confirms what matters

Approvals, extracted-field commits and outbound replies all refuse an AI actor at the code level. Not a policy — a check that throws. Nothing financially consequential leaves without a person.

Provenance on every AI output

Each extracted value carries a confidence score and the verbatim line it was read from. Anything below the threshold is flagged and raises a blocking task rather than quietly becoming fact.

An audit trail you cannot rewrite

Every state change is an append-only event. The shipment's current state is derived from that log, so who changed what, when, and from which source document is answerable months later without anyone's memory.

Model-agnostic by design

The AI layer is an interface with swappable providers, and token cost is attributed per shipment. Run Kambio on one vendor today and another tomorrow without touching the domain logic.

Isolation between customers

Tenancy is enforced at the query layer, not at call sites. Every database read and write is rewritten to scope to the signed-in organisation, and a write naming a different organisation throws rather than succeeding quietly. A developer who forgets to filter still cannot reach another customer's data.

kambio. Where things stand

Built, and honest about the edges.

Kambio is a working product, not a concept. It is also early, and the gaps are worth stating plainly.

Working today
  • Email-in shipment creation with field extraction and provenance
  • The permissioned room, with enforcement at the query layer
  • Scoped counterparty links for CHA, forwarder and buyer
  • AI-drafted replies and cross-party relay, human-confirmed
  • Automated, capped follow-ups on unanswered requests
  • One email thread per shipment per party
  • Append-only event ledger and per-shipment AI cost tracking
Not yet
  • OCR for scanned documents. Text files and PDFs with a text layer extract; photographs of paper do not.
  • Carrier tracking is designed for but not connected.
  • Per-role permissions inside the exporter's own team.
  • WhatsApp works within Meta's messaging window; templates are unregistered.

Deliberately out of scope: trade finance, lending, payments, insurance, marketplace, customs filing.
